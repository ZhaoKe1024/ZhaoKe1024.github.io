**Accepted at KDD 2027.** OPT-MCM evaluates end-to-end optimization agents on open-ended
"near real-world" tasks, where a single objective value cannot decide correctness. Each task is a
quadruple `(p, A_p, D_p, R_p)`: the original statement, a structured task analysis, the attachment
data, and a task-specific yet solution-route-invariant rubric. Rubrics are generated by
**Solution-Rubric Co-Evolution**, which jointly explores multiple modeling paradigms and refines the
evaluation criteria against real solution artifacts, reaching a Spearman correlation of **0.82** with
expert judgments.
