AGEDR introduces the **Attribute Mapping Embedding (AME)** module, which turns scalar
attributes into Gaussian parameters and aligns them with a subset of the VAE latent vector via
via KL alignment, while mutual-information minimization separates the attribute latent vector from the
remaining one. On COUGHVID, AGEDR reaches **97.23% precision / 97.06% AUC** with an SVM head, and
t-SNE shows the sensitive attributes are evenly spread over the latent space (attribute desensitization).
